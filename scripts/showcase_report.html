Methods and sources

Samples come from the all-gene mutation export. Tumors with no reported variants in that export fall outside the sample universe. Repeated reports of an allele in a sample count once.

GDC copy state requires one segment spanning the GRCh38 locus without another overlapping segment. Gain and loss include the cutoff boundaries. Cached GDC segments can resolve samples that the original pilot could not download. Progenetix evidence comes from cached queries joined by audited GDC sample UUID. Beacon intervals are displayed as 1-based inclusive coordinates.

Counts describe same-sample co-occurrence. Sequence calls and copy-number segments do not identify which allele carries an event.
